## Landing motion changes
- Hero: the brain sits a little nearer the middle on wide screens (centre 72% of the width at 1000 px and below, easing to 66% at 1400 px and above); its width is capped at 60% of the screen so it never meets the words at 1024. Phones and tablets are unchanged.
- Network to ring: triangles now leave the network one after another (ordered by particle index, spread across 62% of the scroll distance of the transition) and each winds around the ring's centre in a spiral (a bit over one full turn) before settling in place. The network stays where it is while it empties. Reversing scroll retraces the same paths.
- Ring to grid: new "unspool" transition. Triangles peel off the ring in turn, whip away along the ring's tangent and curve into their grid square (quadratic curve), so the grid fills from the left.
- Grid to lock: compresses to the centre of the screen (not the moving shape centre), then expands into the lock and key.
- How it works: the network, ring and grid point lists are sorted (network and ring by angle, grid left to right) so the index order reads as a sweep. Transitions 2 to 4 compute screen-fixed positions (cloud.js converts the two shapes into the current frame; transitions.js has the paths). Reduced motion is unchanged (it still jumps between stops).
- Checked: lint, build, Playwright frames at 0, 15, 30, 50, 70, 90, 100% forward and back at 1440 and 390, hero at 1440, 1024, 768, 390; frame time during a scripted pass through the three changed transitions was about 16.7 ms median (no slowdown) at both sizes. Not checked: touch devices, low-end hardware.
